# South of The Trenches: Endswood, Aldwyn's Green and Lot 9 redesigned (a plan)

The owner's call (2026-11-14): the whole area south of The Trenches is to
look as if it grew, not as if it was ruled out in rectangles. Endswood,
its west rows, the far west, the common and Lot 9 are redesigned as one
place: an old village the estate was dropped into, on rolling ground,
reached by curving roads, with retro-future brutalist blocks along the
ring road, an underground line and a network of walkways over and under
it all. Only Degen's compound stays exactly where it is.

Nothing is built until the owner signs off a phase; keep this plan up to
date. Drafted from a long round of the owner's answers (below); the
pictures are in `docs/south-shots/`.

## What's wrong today (the review)

- `0-plan-today.webp` (a plan drawn from the data) and `0-air-today.webp`
  (from the air): three estates in three rectangles strung along one
  straight dead-end road (the Close), every building square to the axes,
  the west rows mirrored in pairs, four straight avenues slicing the east
  half into strips, a sea of grey paving, a straight-edged strip of common
  and Lot 9's straight fence.
- Blocks that don't read as homes (the owner's picture, 2026-11-14):
  Hawthorn Terrace's four stacked flat slabs, the three rows of lock-ups,
  Kite House and Harrier Point.

## The rules (in `CLAUDE.md`, the owner's picks)

- **Streets follow the land.**
- **No ruler-straight edges.**
- **Nowhere is a dead end on foot:** every cul-de-sac and crescent ends in
  a footpath through, every terrace row has a back alley and long rows a
  ginnel; cut-throughs for walking and bikes, bollards at each end.

And for this area:

- **The Emporium needn't be the tallest** here (the owner's call): the
  south's tallest block about 20; `CLAUDE.md`'s centrepiece rule says so.
- **Nothing threatening**, as everywhere: the dystopian look is in the
  concrete and the scale, never in violence or anything that attacks you.
- **Made-up names only**, fixed copy, as everywhere.

- **Links** between the spokes, meeting them at angles; **the village's
  streets** curving with the ground; **closes and crescents** off them,
  every one ending in a footpath through.
- **The tall band** along the ring road (z about 325 to 370): the Wall and
  the towers. **The estate** round the Circus and east of it to the

- **The council rows** re-laid in closes north-west of the High Road;
  **Aldwyn's Green** south and west of the Circus on its hill, St Aldwyn's

## The blocks

- **The Wall** (proposed name **The Rampart**): one long block curving
  along the ring road as a sound barrier, rising and falling in height
  (stepping up to about 14 by the Circus), small windows on the road's
  side, galleries on the estate's side, rounded window corners, sky
  gardens along its decks, its stair cores standing out from it.
- **Twin towers with a service core** (proposed: **Harrier Point** and
  **Kite Point**, the old names kept): two towers to about 20, each with
  its lifts and stairs in a separate core beside it, joined by bridges
  every few floors, a flared plant room on top, a small beacon.
- **The stairwell blocks** (Fennel House, Linnet, Marten, Sorrel, Teasel,
  Wren) upgraded: re-sited on the estate's courts and spokes, every one
  with the retro-future detail (rounded corners, curved balcony fronts,
  planters), the stair towers the model the rest follows.
- **The sky network:** covered bridges at two or three levels between the
  Wall, the towers and the stairwell blocks, so you can cross the estate
  without touching the ground; warm strip lights under them at night.
- The "abandoned" feel (Kite House's hoardings) moves to a part of one of
  the kept blocks, emptied for refit; where, to settle in Phase 2.

## The phases (each with the owner's sign-off)

1. **The ground and the roads**, in three steps, each shown to the owner
   (the owner signed the plan off and started it, 2026-11-14):
   - **1a, the street web as data** (built, waiting on the owner's
     sign-off). `south.ts` (DOM-free, not in the city yet, tested in
     `tests/south.test.ts`): the Circus moved to (-82, 418), its five
     spokes (`HIGH_ROAD`, `UNDERHILL`, `CHURCH_ROAD`, `SOUTHFOLD`,
     `ENDSWOOD_ROAD`), Gate Lane past Degen's gates (`HEAD`), Field Lane
     out west, ns1s bent into Endswood Road and ns2s into Marsh Lane (the
     south avenue and ns3 stay straight: the city's arterial with its
     buses and Lot 9's road, and the airport's edge), the links, the
     village's streets, and the closes and crescents grown off them
     (seeded, the same every time; the crescents first) with a footpath
     out of every one (`FOOTPATHS`); `SOUTH_CROSSINGS`; the blocks between
     the streets traced as the faces of the street graph
     (`SOUTH_BLOCKS`, each with its zone, `zoneAt()`); the brook and its
     bridge, the rec ground, St Aldwyn's churchyard on the hill, Lot 9's
     new fence (`LOT9_EDGE`, its west side along the brook), the hedges.
     `streetfaces.ts` (DOM-free) is the kit: smooth lines, the lines
     noded where they meet, the blocks traced (Sunridge's way, made
     general). Shot: `4-phase-1a-street-web.webp`, drawn from the data
     to scale.
   - **1b, into the city:** the street web in the street plan
     (`streetplan.ts`) and drawn (a lazy chunk, roads3d.ts's kit), the
     footpaths as walk-only alleys with bollards; ns1s and ns2s cut at
     their bends; the old estate's streets, blocks and what stands in the
     new roads' way taken up (the removed blocks gone; the kept blocks,
     the old places, the west rows, the bungalows and allotments off
     until their phases re-lay them); Lot 9 inside its new fence, its
     homes and camp packed in; the brook, the hedges and the fields'
     ground; `KEEPS`, ground, lamps, traffic and taxis' routes;
     `tests/snapshot.test.ts` rewritten (the owner's go: everything but
     the compound may move).
   - **1c, the rolling ground:** a height field under the south (as
     Crestmont's `terrain.ts`): the village's hill with St Aldwyn's on
     top, the brook's valley, swells in the fields, level under every
     street, block and place.
2. **The blocks.** The Wall, the twin towers and their cores, the upgraded
   stairwell blocks re-sited, the sky network walked on (`endsdecks.ts`'s
   floors), the estate's courts, decks and grass.
3. **Aldwyn's Green.** The photo's terraces (a new kit: bays, slate roofs,
   chimneys, dormers, front yards, back alleys), the High Road's places,
   St Aldwyn's on its hill, the council rows, the bungalows and the
   allotments re-laid, the rec ground.
4. **Below ground.** The underground's south line (The Trenches, Circus,
   Lot 9, Aldwyn's Green, High Road; later on west and to the airport):
   street entrances, stairs, ticket halls, platforms walked on, the ride
   with the tunnel seen; the underpasses under the ring road, the spokes
   and the bent avenues, into the ticket halls. All in `underground.ts`'s
   one kit.
5. **The exits.** South-west and west, as the ring's others
   (`viaduct.ts`), and their roads in.
6. **Life, night, sound and polish.** People, traffic and buses on the new
   roads, the blocks' lights at night, sound by district, `?bench` views,
   phones checked and trimmed, every width, the guides.

Each phase: `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm test`,
`npm run test:wide`, a build, `npm run budget`, the smoke test, and shots
in `docs/south-shots/`.

## Performance

- A lazy chunk per part (the ground and roads, the blocks, the village,
  below ground), everything fixed through a `Batch` in sites, many-copy
  things instanced (terraced houses, chimneys, bays, planters), near
  detail near only.
- The village's terraces are the heaviest part: one kit, instanced by
  the house, the bays and dormers near only; phones draw every other
  chimney and no dormers.
- Measured with `?fps` and `?bench` before Phase 1 and after every phase,
  on the phone tier.
